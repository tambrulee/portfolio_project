About me

Web, systems, and software — built with a practical eye for real users.

I’m a developer with a background in web design, digital systems, and client-focused problem solving.

Since 2021, I’ve worked across a mix of freelance, e-commerce, and hosted web projects — including Shopify storefronts, responsive business websites, and custom integrations. Alongside client work through platforms like Fiverr, I’ve also worked with website ecosystems including Shopify, Squarespace, and Wix.

More recently, my focus has shifted toward full-stack development and interactive web applications using technologies like React, Next.js, Django, and SQL. I enjoy building systems that feel practical, intuitive, and well considered — combining clean UX with strong technical foundations.

Outside of development, I spend a lot of time hiking in the Lake District, which often influences the kinds of products and ideas I like to build.
